# Median't — photo cleaner: design (project map)

Date: 2026-10-28. Status: approved by the user in the brainstorm ("looks good, code away").
Mockups: `docs/superpowers/mockups/` (HTML fragments from the brainstorm; open in a browser).

## Intent

A Tinder-style photo cleaner for iOS: you go through your photos one by one and swipe **left to
delete**, **right to keep**. The user has paid for apps like this before and is tired of it.
Median't must be **free and useful**.

## Goals

- Fast one-handed cleanup of one album at a time, newest photos first.
- Always clear how much space you are about to free.
- Deleting is safe: swipe-left only marks; real deletion happens in one batch with one iOS
  dialog, and iOS keeps the photos in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Duplicate / similar-photo detection, "smart" suggestions, video compression.
- iPad, landscape, macOS. Accounts, sync, cloud.
- ~~A Settings tab.~~ Added 2026-10-30, see "5. Settings tab".

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it "works")

- Any subscription, paywall, ads, or in-app purchase that unlocks something. Optional tips that
  unlock nothing are allowed (added 2026-10-31, see the tips design). No tracking or analytics SDKs. (Crash
  reports are not tracking: Firebase Crashlytics, added 2026-10-30, sends only crash reports and
  anonymous session counts for crash-free stats — never Firebase Analytics, never what the user does or which photos they see.)
- An iOS deletion dialog after every single swipe.
- Big, clumsy controls. Users are not "blind grandmas": controls are compact, but reachable with
  one thumb (bottom of the screen).
- A second, app-made confirmation dialog on top of the iOS one.

## Platform and how it is made

SwiftUI, iOS 17+, iPhone only, portrait, dark-first (follows system appearance). PhotoKit for the
library. One third-party dependency: Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports, added 2026-10-30;
Firebase project `mediant-ba64c`). Project generated by XcodeGen (`project.yml`).

## Screens and behavior

### Root: floating tab bar

- Three tabs, left to right: **Albums**, **Trash**, **Settings** (gear). Icons only, no text, in a
  small glass capsule at the bottom center (mockup 03).
- The Trash icon has a red badge with the number of photos in the Trash (hidden at 0).
- The swipe screen is pushed on top and hides the tab bar.
- **Swipe between tabs:** a horizontal swipe on the tab bar, or from a page edge that has a tab
  behind it, goes to the next or previous tab (swipe left → the tab on the right). All tabs stay
  alive, so each comes back as it was left; the pages
  slide that way (tabs sit left to right as in the bar). Edge swipes work only on the root pages:
  the swipe screen keeps its own drag, and the left edge of a pushed album is the system back swipe.

### 1. Albums (main page)

- Large title "Albums", subtitle with album count and total size.
- List of the user's albums: thumbnail (newest item), name, **item count**, **storage size**.
- Album sources: smart albums that contain items (Recents, Favorites, Screenshots, Videos, Selfies,
  Live Photos, Portrait, Panoramas, …) and the user's own albums. Empty albums are hidden.
- Order: Recents first; then Favorites, Recently Added, Videos, Screenshots and Selfies, in this
  order (found by smart-album type, not by name, so it works in every language; iOS 18 has no
  Recently Added); then the other albums by name.
- Storage size is calculated in the background and appears when ready (placeholder "…" until
  then). Sizes are cached.
- **Progress card** (added 2026-10-28) pinned at the bottom above the tab bar: all-time
  "space freed" and "items deleted", counted from photos really deleted by "Empty".

### 2. Album grid

- Back button, album name, photos in a 3-column square grid, **newest first** (creation date
  descending).
- Trashed (👹) photos are never shown. Kept (💖) photos are hidden by default; a "Don't show 💖"
  button at the top right is on (pink) by default, and turning it off shows them (the setting is
  remembered; on iOS 26+ it is a system Liquid Glass button). (Changed 2026-11-16.) The swipe screen
  works on exactly the photos the grid shows. (Changed 2026-10-28.)
- Tap a photo → open the swipe screen **starting at that photo**, going to older photos.

### 3. Swipe screen (mockup 02)

- Full-screen photo. Every photo fits inside (aspect-fit), never cropped, over a blurred,
  darkened copy of the same photo.
- **No** place, date or Delete/Keep buttons. Only a small **size label** (e.g. "4.2 MB") at the
  bottom left.
- Top bar: only the "‹ Back" chip (left).
- Bottom row, above the strip, in thumb reach: size label (and the video chips) on the left;
  the **👹 counter chip** (= number of items in the Trash) and the Undo chip on the right.
  **Tapping the 👹 chip opens the Trash tab**; the swipe screen stays behind it (same photo, same
  Undo, the video waits), so swiping or tapping back to Albums returns to it. After "Empty"
  really deletes photos, the swipe screen closes (its photos may be gone). On a video row the size label shrinks first so the
  chips always fit. (Moved down from the top bar 2026-10-29.)
- Undo chip (SF Symbol `arrow.uturn.backward` + "Undo"), same style as the Back chip. Undo reverts the
  last swipe in this session and returns to that photo.
- **Swipe gesture, "card stack" animation:** the photo is a card with round corners that follows
  the finger and tilts (up to 12°, around its bottom edge). The next photo waits behind it,
  smaller and darker, and comes forward as the top card leaves. Release past the threshold →
  the card flies off the screen; otherwise spring back. Undo brings the card back onto the stack.
- **Tap the photo = skip:** the card slides up and away and the next photo comes forward. The
  photo keeps its decision (or stays undecided); a skip is not on the undo stack.
- While dragging: ✨-style sparkles twinkle on the left edge (delete, **#470112**) or the right
  edge (keep, **#75013d**). Their opacity follows drag progress; when the swipe ends they keep
  twinkling and fade out softly (about 0.7 s). Sparkles are pointy, tall
  four-point stars like ✨, some in small 3-star clusters, upright while twinkling.
- **Preview strip** at the bottom: small previews of the album's photos in their real aspect
  ratio; the current one is in the middle, bigger, with a white frame; older photos to the right.
  - Kept: 💖 in the bottom-right corner; it pops the same way as 👹 when you swipe right.
  - Trashed: darkened tile with 👹 in the bottom-right corner. When you swipe left, the 👹 fades
    in (opacity 0→1, fast) at the tile's full size, then shrinks into the corner (~0.6 s).
  - Tapping a preview jumps to that photo. Dragging the strip scrolls it (with a fling and a
    soft bounce at the ends); on release, the preview nearest the middle becomes the current photo.
- After the last (oldest) photo: a short "All done" state with Undo, the 👹 chip and a button back
  to the album.

### 4. Trash tab (mockup 03)

- Large title "Trash", subtitle "N photos · X MB to free".
- Grid of all trashed items (from all albums), with 👹 in the corner.
- Bottom row, one line, thumb reach: **"Select"** chip (left) — tab bar (center) — red
  **"Empty"** chip (right).
- **Empty** → directly the iOS system deletion dialog for all items (no app dialog). On success
  the Trash is cleared; on "Don't Allow" nothing changes.
- **Select** → select mode: tap items to select; bottom row becomes "Cancel" (left) and
  "Restore (n)" (right). Restore takes items out of the Trash (they become undecided).
- Empty state: a short friendly message. Once anything was deleted, it reminds the user to also
  delete the photos from Recently Deleted in Photos (iOS keeps them there for 30 days and they
  still take space), with an "Open Photos" chip (`photos-redirect://`; iOS has no public link to
  the Recently Deleted album itself). Before the first delete: "Swipe left on photos you don't want."
- **Just emptied (added 2026-10-31):** right after "Empty" really deleted photos (not after "Don't
  Allow", a failed delete or a restore), the empty state shows a drawn pink ✨, "Done!", the size
  freed by that delete ("1.2 GB freed", left out if unknown), "48 photos deleted", the Recently
  Deleted hint and "Open Photos", then a thin divider, the quiet line "Liked it? Treat the
  developer ✨" and four pink tip chips (the App Store prices). A tip shows "Thank you! ✨" for
  about 3 s; if the products cannot load, the whole tip part is hidden. The state ends when the
  user leaves the Trash tab, the app goes to the background, or new photos land in the Trash; it
  is not saved. Details: `2026-10-31-settings-tips-design.md`.

### 5. Settings tab (added 2026-10-30)

- Large title "Settings", then glass cards in the style of the progress card:
  - **Language:** "English" / "Русский" chips (the chosen one pink). The app changes at once and
    remembers the choice; without a choice it follows the phone's language. Only the app's own
    texts change: iOS dialogs (photo access, delete) and album names from Photos stay in the
    phone's language. Numbers and sizes keep the phone's region format; Russian sizes use МБ/ГБ.
  - **Trash reminder:** "On" / "Off" chips (on by default). When iOS blocks notifications, the row
    says so and offers "Open Settings" (the app's iOS notification settings).
  - **Tip card (added 2026-10-31):** between Trash reminder and Progress. A card in the icon art
    (diagonal split `#470112` / `#75013d`), "Treat the developer ✨", a short text ("Median't is
    free ... It unlocks nothing.") and four tip buttons with the App Store prices (one row, or 2 × 2
    when the prices are long). Tips are consumable in-app purchases that unlock nothing; success
    plays fireworks over the whole app and a haptic tap per burst. No popup, badge or reminder.
  - **Progress:** "Reset" (red) sets "space freed" and "items deleted" back to zero after a
    confirmation sheet; disabled while both are zero. Photos do not change.
  - **Version (moved 2026-10-31):** no longer a card. A small capsule "Median't 1.0 (12)" is
    pinned above the tab bar on this tab; tap or long-press copies it ("Copied" for 1 s).
  - Order: Language, Trash reminder, Tip card, Progress.
- Later (see TODO): about the creator.
- Texts live in `Localizable.xcstrings` (English + Russian with plural forms) and
  `InfoPlist.xcstrings` (the photo access text).

### 6. Trash reminder (added 2026-10-30)

- One **local** notification (no server, no push entitlement; works with the free signing team):
  leaving the app with photos in the Trash schedules it **2 hours** later (Debug builds: 30 s, for
  trying it on a phone); coming back cancels it. Text, fixed when scheduled, in the app language:
  "12 photos (340 MB) wait in the Trash. Empty it to free the space." Tapping it opens the Trash.
- No nag: the same set of Trash photos is reminded once; a changed Trash can be reminded again.
- Permission: iOS asks the first time the Trash tab opens with photos in it — never at launch,
  never during swiping. Turning the reminder on in Settings asks too, if iOS has not asked yet.
- Opt-out: the Settings switch, or iOS Settings › Notifications.

## Data and rules

- Swipe left = add the asset to the **Trash**; swipe right = mark **kept**. Both are stored by
  PhotoKit `localIdentifier` and persisted on disk, so they survive app restarts.
- On launch / library change, identifiers that no longer exist are dropped.
- A photo can be in only one state: undecided, kept, or trashed.
- Undo works on the current swipe session history (stack of decisions).
- Real deletion only via **Empty** (`PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets`), in one batch.
- Sizes: per-asset size from `PHAssetResource` (sum of the asset's resources; the
  `fileSize` value via KVC — the only practical source; fall back to "—" if missing).

## Permissions

- Ask for full Photos read-write access on first launch with a short explanation screen.
- Limited access: works with the photos the user allowed; show a small hint to allow more.
- Denied: explanation + button that opens iOS Settings.

## Builder's calls (decided by Claude, not by the user — check these)

- Videos are included and follow the same rules as photos (swipe, tap to skip, aspect-fit). The
  top card plays its video in a loop; the card behind shows the poster frame, and the poster also
  shows while the video loads. Muted, the app follows the silent switch and leaves the user's
  music playing (ambient). With the sound on it plays even when the phone is on silent
  (playback). A volume-up press while a video plays muted turns the sound on, the same as the
  🔊 chip (no event comes when the volume is already at maximum). At the bottom left: a "⏸ 0:12 / 0:32" chip whose light fill shows the played part
  (tap it to pause or play, the icon shows what a tap does; drag it sideways to scrub: the video
  stops while you drag, a 300 pt drag covers the whole video; a paused video stays paused after a
  scrub; the next video always starts playing), a 🔊/🔇
  chip (muted by default, remembered between launches; a tap on the card is skip, so sound needs
  its own chip),
  and the size. Video tiles in the album grid show "▶ 0:32" in the bottom-left corner.
- Kept state is persisted and shown as 💖 in the strip and grid.
- Tapping a preview in the strip jumps to it.
- "Select" in the Trash restores items (the user confirmed this guess implicitly with
  "looks good").
- Album sizes are cached and recalculated in the background when the library changes.
- Custom floating tab bar instead of the system `TabView` bar, to match the mockup.
- Haptic feedback: light tick on commit, success haptic after Empty.
